# Evaluation

Google reports top foundation-model ranking on GIFT-Eval, FEV-Bench, and TIME;
read those as vendor claims unless independently reproduced.[^blog]

The repository provides runners, notebooks, and results paths for all three
benchmarks. The collected benchmark pages are time-sensitive reference checks,
not a durable guarantee of current rank.[^assets][^fev][^gift][^time]
